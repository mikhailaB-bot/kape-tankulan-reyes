How to Order

Ordering from Kape Tankulan Reyes is easy! Follow these simple steps to get your favorite coffee delivered to your doorstep.

  1. Visit our Menu and choose your favorite coffee.
  2. Select your preferred size and customization.
  3. Place your order with our staff.
  4. Pay for your order at the counter.
  5. Wait for your freshly prepared drink.

We are happy to serve you! Thank you for Supporting Kape Tankulan Reyes.
